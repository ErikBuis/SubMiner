- The sentence in the card review is editable, like the translation: click it, or `Enter` on it, to type. While typing, `Enter` adds a line break and `Escape` stops typing without closing the dialog. A click puts the caret where you clicked, and a drag still just selects text.
- Once edited, the sentence shows as typed (without readings) and the line count says "edited". Added or removed lines go in or out at its start or end; a removed line you rewrote stays. Typing it back to the original, clearing it, or Reset all brings back the subtitle lines.
- The edited sentence is what the card gets, also when exporting without media. The card keeps the translation the review shows, instead of a new lookup against the text you rewrote.
