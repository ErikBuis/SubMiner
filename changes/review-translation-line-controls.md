- The media timing review has Prev and Next `−`/`+` buttons on the translation, to add or drop secondary subtitle lines at its start or end, independent of the lines on the card sentence. Lines that only run alongside the dialogue, like a TV in the background, are skipped.
- A translation you edited in the review keeps your text when you add or remove lines: the line is put in or cut at its start or end instead of the edit being dropped or overwritten. A removed line is only cut while your text still starts or ends with it as whole words; otherwise your text stays exactly as you typed it.
